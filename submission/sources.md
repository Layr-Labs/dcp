## Reproduced PPC interfaces and their limits

proof.md Section 1 reproduces PPC lines 56-210 for exact transforms, the latent
label model, numerical extraction, fixed precision and source charges.
Sections 6 and 7 reproduce PPC lines 581-814 for the all-N decoder, finite
branch, two literal caps and the unconditional coupling/union bound. They
use F4's q0, all-D certificates, and two-copy base. Sections 2-5 and 8-9 prove
the changed interface, costs and profile comparison; they do not cite PPC's
one-qubit inductive uniformity as a substitute for the four-row proof.

PPC itself credits CYG1 for its supplier and free-frequency decoder, and PPSA
for partial-pivot scalar recovery. That inherited credit is retained in the
reproduced text. The direct byte-exact source available here is the submitted
PPC proof above, not an assertion that earlier versions were independently
retrieved or that the earlier Opus review certified this F4 construction.

The earlier PPSA Opus review is archived as PPSA-review-context.md for scope context only.
Its required sample-scope correction was already present in the submitted
PPC proof. Its verdict is not used as evidence that this proof is correct.

The parent's REPORT.md, HANDOFF.md, scripts and results are preserved as
parent-prefixed dependencies. Their finite diagnostics and public snapshots
are historical context, not all-N proof. They were inspected but not run in
their original directory, so their results remain unchanged. Newly written
bounded scripts check the F4 artifact separately. The author did not refresh public status during the integration task.
The parent later refreshed 246 public rows and verified the six declared
public source commits before preparing this package. No new frontier
dominance is claimed.

The five-claim representative vector retains PPC's rational coefficients.
The genuine change is the executed f(b), four-entry instrument and profile,
with the same-base analytic saving in proof.md Section 9. Tightening rational
envelope slack alone would not count as a new mechanism or profile result.

## Relocated provenance from the reviewed proof


All nine construction claims in the unpublished parent seed were audited
as claims, not premises. Sections 2.3-2.4 prove the coherent tuple instrument
and give explicit old-index cleanup. Sections 2.1-2.2 add the common-residue
carry circuit. Sections 3-4 establish the restricted conditional moments,
qubitized-label law, survival, rational local work and termination together.
Sections 5-8 supply two-copy multiplicities, every-D maxima, literal caps,
source/decoder costs and the unconditional success union bound. Section 9
proves the proposed same-base profile separation. No seed parameter was
changed. The accounting word was fixed at 1024(n+1) and the local mean
certificate at 512 to make explicit implementation allowances easy to audit.

The immediate unpublished input is dcp-multi-endpoint/PROOF-seed.md, including
its fixed-position quarter qubitization and retained-tuple proposal. PPC
supplies scalar pair-failure recovery, the dyadic schedule, triangular-query
idea, source extraction, decoder, critical-capacity method and cap framework.
Its source and decoder arguments are reproduced in Sections 1,6,7; the
changed claims are proved afresh in the other sections.

Corrected MWQ restricts its moment lemma to low bits and uses fresh-attempt
renewal. GVQ3 already uses distinct binary-incidence phase vectors, common
low residues and direct planted moments. These are material conceptual
sources. The original MWG Lemma 8.2 incorrectly conditions on all prior
completed tables; that statement is rejected and not used. WINDOW and
DENSE are comparison-only sources for existing cyclic/ranked-list
instruments. The present extractor does not use their full-fiber/list rank
maps. No worldwide novelty claim is made. Exact versions, hashes and line
citations, including the unpublished input, are in sources.md.
