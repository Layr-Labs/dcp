# Direct angular score with a private probe for each copy

Let N>=2, n=ceil(log2 N), K=2^58 n and B=2^32. Acquire exactly K source copies. The tolerated fraction is 333333/1000000. For each run either the positions and joint input are fixed before private randomness, with the exact ideal product marginal on all unmarked positions and arbitrary joint extension, or an adversary chooses malformed positions before each delivery using all earlier outcomes. In the adaptive case, each clean delivery is a fresh independent ideal state in tensor product with the workspace and the adversary, and malformed deliveries are arbitrary states of the required shape, possibly correlated through the adversary. In each case at most floor(epsilon(n) Q) deliveries are malformed on every branch and the adversary has no access to private coins before a delivery or to the workspace. The algorithm is given no copy-validity flags. Every branch returns one residue modulo N. Use the native Fourier-label instrument in BASE13 Section 3 with its precision cap set to K, followed by X on the row to give the positive phase convention. For an ideal copy, its hidden Fourier label k is uniform modulo N and the row phase is exp(2 pi i k s/N). The label instrument touches only the position system before the row operations. The Section 3 coupling compares the full clean label transcript to this ideal report with discrepancy probability at most 2^-14 over at most K clean-copy calls. On any bad or padded transcript, use the instrument's deterministic rounding convention to report an integer in [0,N), using 0 if the convention reports an invalid result. Every row measurement has sign Z in {-1,1}.

After each copy's label readout and fixed row X gate, use an independent uniform b in {0,...,B-1} from 32 fair bits. Put beta=2 pi b/B. Measure the row sign in the beta equatorial axis, with an exactly executable approximation obtained as follows. A rotation diag(1,exp(-i beta)) before X measurement has the desired ideal law, with conditional mean cos(2 pi k s/N-beta). Generate a Clifford+T circuit whose induced sign observable differs from the target observable by at most 2^-32 in operator norm. One uniform fixed-cost implementation enumerates all words in H,T and the inverses and all finite rational certification precisions, in increasing total description length. For certification, accept once the maximum absolute entry difference of the two 2-by-2 sign observables has a certified upper bound below 2^-33. This implies operator-norm difference below 2^-32. Every tested matrix is computed with convergent rational intervals, and the target trigonometric constants have convergent Taylor interval enclosures. Test only finitely many words and precisions at each length and increase the bound until a word is accepted. Projective single-qubit universality of H and T ensures termination for each of the 2^32 fixed targets. Thus all generation work and the lengths of the words have absolute finite bounds independent of N and of input outcomes. The controller generates only the selected circuit after reading b, and pays for generation and execution on every record. No table of all words is needed. The bounded number of words examined for any of the fixed 2^32 targets is a constant in n. Every synthesis depends only on the private fixed-width angle index. A reference for the finite single-qubit gate approximation principle is Dawson and Nielsen, *The Solovay-Kitaev algorithm*, arXiv:quant-ph/0505030.

Store each record (k,b,Z), discarding both source registers. Given a candidate a in [0,N), define its term to be

    Z * sign_+(cos(2 pi (ka mod N)/N - beta)),

where sign_+(x) returns +1 when x>=0, and -1 otherwise. This is an exact discrete function of three integers. Take u=(B*(ka mod N)-b*N) modulo BN in [0,BN), then test 4u<= BN or 4u>=3BN for the positive sign, including both endpoints. Cross products here take O(n) bits, and the multiplications other than ka are by a fixed word or a fixed-length b. No numerical cosine is evaluated by the scorer. Use normalized average of the K terms and mark iff it is at least 21220659/100000000.

## Clean means and simultaneous concentration

For fixed angles x=2 pi k s/N and y=2 pi k a/N, the continuously averaged ideal term is

    (1/(2 pi)) integral_0^(2 pi) cos(x-beta) sign_+(cos(y-beta)) d beta
      = (2/pi) cos(x-y).

Indeed shift beta by y, expand the cosine addition identity, and use the zero sine integral by reflection. The remaining integral of |cos| is 4. On the public grid, compare the equally spaced left-endpoint sum to this integral using B equal half-open cells. Away from the two possible jump locations the integrand is 1-Lipschitz. A jump exactly at a grid endpoint can affect the adjoining cell starting at that endpoint when the tie sign disagrees with the right limit; if it is a right endpoint it does not affect the preceding cell. Thus at most two cells incur an extra worst-case discrepancy of 2/B each. The total discrepancy is at most (2 pi+4)/B. Native measurement synthesis changes the expectation by at most 2^-32, so the combined discrepancy is less than 1/100000000 using pi<22/7. Taking the uniform Fourier-label average, the correct candidate has mean at least 2/pi-1/100000000 > 63661976/100000000. For an incorrect candidate, the complex geometric sum of exp(2 pi i k(s-a)/N) is zero, so its mean is at most 1/100000000. Machin's identity with ten terms of each alternating arctangent series and its next-term error gives 63661977/100000000 < 2/pi < 63661978/100000000 by exact rational comparison. These averages concern the ideal-label experiment with the actual synthesized row circuits.

Fix the malformed set and consider the reduced marginal of the clean systems only. Each local record instrument uses independent private bits and acts only on its own copy. Clean records are independent with the ideal-label law after coupling; no conditioning on malformed records is involved. Hoeffding gives failure probability at most 2 exp(-K/(2*(2^27)^2)) for the clean score sum of any fixed candidate to differ from its ideal expectation by more than K/2^27, because the clean count is at most K and each contribution ranges in [-1,1]. Add the unconditional label coupling discrepancy 2^-14 once for the entire clean record list.

For each malformed record, condition on the full preceding physical transcript and its own completed label readout. The conditional row state is some qubit density matrix even when that row is entangled with other systems. Its ideal sign expectation at axis beta has the form v_x cos(beta)+v_y sin(beta)=r cos(x-beta), with 0<=r<=1 by the Bloch disk bound. The b choice is private, uniform and sampled after the label readout, so this conditional state and the value y=2 pi k a/N do not depend on b. The same continuous integral and grid comparison give absolute conditional expected score at most 2/pi+1/100000000 < 63661979/100000000. Synthesis error and arbitrary readout labels are included. Averaging over the label subtranscript preserves both endpoints of the bound when conditioning just on the preceding record transcript.

The sum of the malformed contributions minus the sum of their conditional means is a martingale sum. Use all record indices, setting the increment to zero on clean positions. Conditioned on the preceding transcript, each nonzero increment is centered and lies in an interval of length two. The exponential-moment form of Hoeffding for each conditional increment gives the same bound 2 exp(-K/(2*(2^27)^2)) for exceeding K/2^27 in absolute value as for the clean sum. To see this, each increment has conditional moment at most exp(lambda^2/2), so iteration over at most K increments and Markov at lambda=1/2^27 give the claim. This uses the actual physical transcript, not the ideal coupling. The two bounds can therefore be combined by union without conditioning one on the other. Union over all valid candidates and both sums gives probability at most 2^(n+2) exp(-8n) < 1/128 for n>=1.

For the adaptive source model instead enlarge the filtration to include the current delivery decision just before its entirely local measurement instrument. The still-fresh clean delivery, if chosen, has the true and false conditional mean bounds above with an additional additive 2^-13/K from the native label approximation (Section 3). Here a label mismatch changes a term by at most two. The total effect of these additional errors after normalization is less than 2^-27. Both the masked clean and masked malformed contributions, each centered at its own conditional mean given prior transcript and the present delivery status, obey the same conditional Hoeffding bound. Formally, insert a filtration step for revealing the delivery status with zero increment, followed by the score step with increment zero in the other category. This uses no conditioning on the final corruption count or on future statuses. Under either model the combined normalized deviation is at most 3/2^27, except with total probability at most 1/128+2^-14. The 2^-14 term is only needed for the fixed-position label comparison.

Write f=m/K<=333333/1000000 for the actual malformed fraction. On these concentration events and the clean label coupling, the normalized correct score is at least

    (1-333333/1000000)*(63661976/100000000) - (333333/1000000)*(63661979/100000000) - 3/2^27 > 21220659/100000000.

Every incorrect normalized score is at most

    (1-333333/1000000)*(1/100000000) + (333333/1000000)*(63661979/100000000) + 3/2^27 < 21220659/100000000.

Both inequalities are exact rational comparisons. The first expression decreases as f increases and the second increases as f increases. Hence the endpoint bounds apply, and precisely one valid candidate is marked. The bounds apply even if earlier outcomes affect the conditional state of a malformed row by entanglement. The same deterministic endpoint comparison applies to either corruption model and any realized terminal corruption count.

## Counted search

Use an n-qubit uniform candidate register over A=2^n, with padding (a>=N) never marked. Compute ka modulo N using the exact linear-space reversible multiplication and reciprocal reduction in fast-score-arithmetic.md. The sign test uses exact integer comparisons. With the stored sign, update a signed O(log(n+2))-bit sum, then reverse the per-record scratch calculation. After all K terms apply the exact threshold 100000000 sum >=21220659 K under the valid-index flag. Apply the sign flip, then reverse the full evaluation including the padding flag. Each modular multiplication costs O(n^(5/4)) indexed work and uses O(n) qubits. The other fixed-width O(n)-bit operations use ripple logic with address and loop cursor charges. Each has O(n polylog(n+2)) work, which is absorbed into O(n^(5/4)). Scan records through classical addresses and reuse the arithmetic space. A classical postmeasurement mark check can instead perform ordinary schoolbook arithmetic.

An oracle and its emitted inverse cost O(K n^(5/4)) total sequential work. Classical postmeasurement verification using schoolbook arithmetic costs polynomial work per trial. There are O(n) doubling scales and 128 trials per scale, so all such checks together are polynomial and not a per-Grover-iteration expense. The classical preprocessing, indexed gates, loop arithmetic, and stored circuit descriptions are charged in the arithmetic proof.

For the search use a fixed sequence of scales M=1,2,4,... ending at the first power of two with M^2>=16A. At each scale run 128 independent trials, selecting uniform j in [0,M), applying j Grover iterations from the uniform start, and measuring and checking the output. Stop on the first verified mark, or output 0 at the fixed end. One Grover step uses the exact phase oracle and H^n followed by an all-zero sign flip and H^n, with a reusable linear Toffoli chain. For marked fraction p, p>=1/2 succeeds at scale 1 with probability at least 1/2 per trial. For 0<p<1/2 let sin^2(theta)=p. At a scale M, averaging sin^2((2j+1)theta) over uniform j gives 1/2-sin(4M theta)/(4M sin(2theta)), by the finite geometric sum. Since p>=1/A and sin(2theta)>=sqrt(2/A), the first scale M>=2/sin(2theta) is included. At that scale each trial has success probability at least 3/8, so the failure probability is at most (5/8)^128<2^-32. Summing the doubling scales gives O(sqrt(A)) iterations even on an all-failure branch. For p=0 the same cap still applies. No trial calls the source or its inverse.

The bad-event budget 1/128+2^-14+2^-32 is below 1/8. All guarantees are simultaneous. The stored records and controller scratch fit O(n^2) classical bits. The one source copy and native label circuit, candidate register, modular arithmetic and diffusion use O(n) qubits, reused. Native readout on K copies has polynomial work; the charged oracle work times O(2^(n/2)) trials and iterations gives O(2^(n/2) n^(9/4)) sequential work, with log(n+2) at finite short lengths. K=2^58 n is the exact acquisition budget on every branch. The work growth index is 1, and the other indices are zero. Fraction 333333/1000000 is a supported constant and has no independent time score.
