# Subsampled pair buckets

The main branch is n>=1024. Set Bmax=floor(n/3), h=n-Bmax, d0=floor(log2 N), and B=d0-h. These choices give Bmax-1<=B<=Bmax. Use the odd-part and high-valuation split from terminal-block.md at this actual B and h. In the odd branch (v2(N)<B), use its public CRT multiplier in each odd stage, and truncate cyclic Fourier labels to the centered interval [-2^(d0-1),2^(d0-1)). In the high-valuation branch, use the first direct stage modulo N/2^B after truncating labels to [0,2^d0). Residual stages in either branch have N=M0*2^D, e=min(D,h), and b=D-e cancellation bits. In those stages use the full canonical labels k, with logical labels k mod 2^D, as in root-slots.md. The initial odd or direct stage's cancellation parameter is b=B. Residual cancellation has 0<=b<=Bmax. The stages and their sizes are determined by N, independent of all outputs. Stage stride is h-4>=n/2 on the main range, so the bounded odd rounding rule needs at most three stages. There are at most three residual stages and at most one first direct stage. Use the public bound S<=16 on the total stage count.

For n<1024 take a single direct stage modulo N with b=0, Bmax=0, and no truncation or CRT multiplier. Its ideal native X/Y score has mean 1/2 for the true candidate and zero for every wrong candidate by the uniform-label geometric sum. This finite branch uses the same acquisition, robustness and search rules below. Put M=2^(Bmax+ceil(log2 n)+28), a power of two, and K=2^40 n. Acquire M copies in each stage exactly, regardless of past outcomes. Use the shortened native label instrument of short-readout.md. The exact total acquired copies are Q=SM. After the local position operations and readout use the positive row convention from BASE13. The score record collection depends on N and the current stage but not on an earlier decoded prefix. At the score step the prefix determines only a known arithmetic phase offset or a search interval. In particular no source preparation uses a candidate produced by another stage.

## An individual round and the retained physical registers

A stage at cancellation b>=1 uses M/2^b consecutive rounds of exactly 2^b copies. Each round has 2^b cells indexed by low b bits of an accepted logical label. Every raw readout either has an accepted label and cell or is discarded by the public truncation rule, with all readout outputs in [0,N). Each cell produces a record only if it receives exactly two accepted rows. On its two rows measure the computational-basis parity by CNOT from the first to the second and measure the second in Z. On even parity emit the inert record; on odd parity keep the first qubit, whose relative phase is the first phase divided by the second phase. This direction convention defines the signed difference label. Its low cancellation follows because both logical labels are in the same cell. The cell record is that signed difference (with both full and logical columns when needed), its independent fair X/Y basis and measured row sign. Cells of any occupancy other than two emit the inert record. On exactly two ideal inputs, odd parity has probability 1/2 independently of the labels. For b=0 use M single-copy cells without pairing. Use no truncation in such a stage, and measure the row directly. For the chosen main widths a b=0 stage can only be residual, so this condition agrees with its unshifted phase law.

Choose K independent uniform cell indices in [0,M) using exact private fair bits, with replacement, before the first readout of the current stage. This indexes the ordered concatenation of all its rounds' cell ranges, and for b=0 indexes the raw copy positions. The implemented algorithm stores only these sampled indices, their multiplicities by repeated occurrences, and each selected cell's counter and at most two rows, labels and eventual record. On a third hit discard that cell's saved rows, and saturate its occupancy at three. Unselected rows are discarded as soon as their native labels are processed. At a round's end finish the selected cells in that round. Duplicated cell indices refer to a single physical cell and the same measured record. Use a simple scan of the stored list for addresses, and serve repeated index occurrences by copying the final measured classical record. No coherent search acts on a cell address. Sampling in advance is valid even on the correlated malformed inputs: an analytical full-pool experiment can apply all native label instruments first, then all separate cell instruments determined from the resulting labels. On each joint branch the selected cell instruments and all the label instruments have the same joint law in the physical retained computation. Discarding unselected cell systems is the partial trace of their optional nonselective cell operations, and deferred operations on selected cells commute with all later local readouts on other copies. Neither label extraction nor cell selection controls those later instruments. Thus sampling only selected outputs in advance has the marginal law of an independent sample from the analytical full physical pool. That pool is an analysis device and is never allocated in the implemented algorithm.

## Coupling to a complete clean pool

Fix a malformed set and allowed joint input. For each clean position, the good Fourier label can be viewed as a latent classical label even in the joint calculation: the native readout acts through translations diagonal in that Fourier basis, and tracing its position register kills off-diagonal Fourier terms. Projecting the latent label before the local tests therefore leaves all accessible output laws unchanged. The good label vector is independent uniform by the ideal product marginal. Given these latent labels, the entire set of clean row qubits has a pure product state fixed by those labels, including the public phase multipliers. Consequently conditioning on any malformed label outcomes or their private systems cannot change those pure clean rows. This conclusion does not require malformed labels to be independent of the latent clean labels. For each clean report the probability of a different label is at most 2^-54 by short-readout.md. If E is the number of incorrect clean reports in a stage, its unconditional expectation is at most M*2^-54, and Markov gives Pr[E>M*2^-25]<=2^-29. This bound uses the actual input law and needs no independence between those discrepancies and the bad systems.

Construct a virtual all-clean pool by using these latent labels and clean rows at good positions, and filling malformed positions with independent ideal labels and their matching ideal rows. This pool has the independent ideal input law in each stage. Treat every maladjusted raw report, whether from a malformed system or a good label discrepancy, as a replaced position. Replacing that input changes at most two cell occupancies or contents, namely its old and new cell when applicable. Count both even if the same cell retains occupancy but its label changes. A b=0 stage changes at most one cell. All remaining cells apply the identical quantum instrument to the same pure good rows with the same phases. Their record outcomes can be coupled identically. To formalize this coupling, condition first on the real and virtual label outcomes and couple the identical instruments of unchanged cells. Given the true labels the rows used in these cells are fixed pure products, so the record marginals agree even conditional on the other labels. Extend the virtual and actual outcomes in changed cells by their respective conditional laws. No need exists to apply two instruments to the same physical row. This construction preserves both full-pool marginal laws. In particular virtual concentration is analyzed in the unconditional all-clean law, and the mismatch event is added to the failure bound rather than conditioning the virtual experiment on it.

## Clean pool size and score margins

For an ideal round with B0=2^b>=2, a given cell receives each raw copy independently with probability p in [1/(2B0),1/B0]. Uniform low bits follow from accepting an interval of length 2^d0 in the truncated branches or from the logical uniform label in a residual branch. In truncated branches the acceptance probability is at least 1/2. Thus the probability of exactly two hits is at least

    binom(B0,2) (1/(2B0))^2 (1-1/B0)^(B0-2) >= 1/64.

Here binom(B0,2)>=B0^2/4 and the last power is >=exp(-1)>1/4 using log(1-x)>=-x/(1-x). Let Y be the number of cells with exactly two inputs in the stage. Its expectation is at least M/64. Replacing one raw label changes at most two cell occupancy indicators. The bounded-difference inequality therefore gives Pr[Y<M/128]<=exp(-M/32768), by independent virtual raw labels. Conditional on just the accepted statuses and the low labels, parity successes of these Y disjoint pairs are independent fair bits, independent of the unused high blocks. The chance fewer than Y/4 yield odd parity is at most exp(-Y/8) by Hoeffding. Thus except for these tails at least W=M/512 cells emit a noninert record. For b=0 all virtual cells emit noninert records.

Conditional on the low labels and parity variables of the virtual pool, the unused high blocks are independent uniform on the corresponding binary intervals. The label after odd parity is a difference of two independent highs times 2^b, with the matching common low value canceled. Its normalized difference W' has characteristic modulus at most the single-block geometric bound D_A, A=2^h in the first stages, and E[(W')^2]<=A^2/6 in the centered case. For odd decoding the public multiplier and known major modulus give frequency 2^(j*stride) W'. The original odd-stage grid with nominal support 2 has the same near-mean lower bound 999/2000 from its centered second moment. At any far candidate in a correct-prefix range the same geometric bound gives mean at most 3/8, since h is large (in fact the bound is 1/16). The fact an interval endpoint can contain the nearest grid point is as in terminal-block.md. For the high-valuation first direct stage, the characteristic bound with A=2^h gives wrong mean at most 1/(2 sqrt(2))<3/8. For residual stages, the normalized logical difference modulo 2^e is uniform, whether obtained from a pair or from a b=0 leaf. If the prefix c is correct modulo M0, the full label difference F gives candidate phase argument F(c+M0*t)/N. The difference from the true phase reduces to F(u-t)/2^D when s=c+M0*u, so the true next e bits have mean 1/2 and every other candidate has mean zero. These are the exact reductions of terminal-block.md and root-slots.md with one simple pair instead of a recursive tree.

For every noninert record define its score to be its usual rounded X/Y cosine contribution minus theta=7/16. Use the exact rational table as in the archives, with error <=1/128, and clip its cosine value to [-1,1]. For every inert cell define its score to be zero. Every score lies in [-1-theta,1-theta], an interval of length two that contains zero. For any fixed near candidate which is a nearest point to the secret angle, the ideal virtual conditional mean in a noninert cell is at least 1/32. For each far candidate it is at most -1/32. The inequalities follow from 999/2000-1/128-7/16>1/32 and 3/8+1/128-7/16<-1/32. Direct true versus wrong has the same respective margins. Noninert cell scores of any fixed candidate are mutually independent under the virtual law given the low transcript and parity outcomes, using disjoint uniform high labels and independent basis and row measurements. Hoeffding deviation at most 1/64 per averaged noninert record fails with probability <=2 exp(-W/8192)<=2 exp(-M/2^22). Thus the full virtual pool average over M cells is >=2^-15 for the needed near or true candidate and <=-2^-15 for all far or wrong candidates. A union bound suffices over the full odd dyadic grids (exponent <=3n as in root-slots.md, with support constant), and all direct extensions over every prefix (<=2^n). This yields simultaneity over later chosen intervals and candidates. Including occupancy and parity failure tails, the union failure for these bounds over S<=16 stages is below 1/256, since M>=2^28 n and there are at most 2^(3n) tested candidates per stage. The finite branch has the same or smaller union bound.

Each stage contains at most the total malformed count m<= (1/2^24) S M. Except with probability at most S*2^-29, every stage has E<=M*2^-25. The actual full pool then differs from the virtual mean by at most 4*(m+E)/M<2^-17 for every candidate, using at most two changed cells per replaced position and the length-two score interval. Given the full actual pool, its K independent uniform cell samples estimate every candidate's mean to accuracy 2^-18 except for probability at most 2 exp(-K/(2*2^36)) for each candidate. Union over the same public families and stages gives failure below 1/256 as K=2^40 n. These conditional sample estimates are applied to the full-pool law already shown to be realized by the marginal retained computation. Define a mark for every valid candidate with positive sampled average score. The joint event supplies a true-near or true mark in each correct-prefix range, and excludes far or wrong marks. Apply the original exact bounded Grover trials in coherent-search.md, with 128 trials per scale. Failure when a mark exists is at most 2^-32 per stage. This advances the true-prefix induction and returns the full secret. Use bounded checks and default 0 on failure, keeping every scheduled acquisition. All probabilistic failures sum to less than 1/8 for the exact declared malformed model.

## Uniform costs on every branch

Use short-readout.md and fast-score-arithmetic.md for native labels, public source permutations, selected-cell lookup and modular score arithmetic. These replace the original schoolbook implementations where specified while leaving the bucket protocol, exact score, branch caps and recovery unchanged. Each source acquisition and the subsequent processing take O(n^(5/4)) work per copy, inclusive of logarithmic address charges and cell bookkeeping; all once-per-stage preprocessing is polynomial. Each coherent predicate costs O(n^(9/4)) work since K=O(n) and each modular product takes O(n^(5/4)) indexed work. Classical checks of measured candidates use ordinary polynomial arithmetic, with at most polynomially many checks. There are O(2^(h/2)) total Grover iterations for the O(2^h) bounded candidate grids, with constant S and fixed trial repetition. Since 2^(h/2)=O(2^(n/3)) and Q=SM=O(2^(n/3)n), total work is O(2^(n/3)n^(9/4)). Actual selected records, label columns and cell indices occupy O(n^2) classical bits, plus O(n) qubits for the saved rows and local circuits. Both memories are reused between stages. The finite branch needs only the same direct procedures, is branchwise bounded, and fits the claimed asymptotic envelopes. The source bound, error guarantee, and all resource bounds hold simultaneously on the same input model and algorithm.
