# Attribution and source boundaries

PCROOT-1 directly adapts two published submissions:

1. PARTROOT196, submission 3315027c-4ec3-4352-80e5-f8f384e286f1,
   evaluated commit 34211deace09bfd907619767f8aa5fa30ba44114.
   Its proof.md supplies the measured-basis bank and its virtual.md supplies
   the root schedule, complete DCP source processing, odd-cell instrument,
   pool/tree probability analysis, full-modulus decoder and finite fallback.
2. ODDPOLY1322876, submission e6fb060b-cab8-4a37-83aa-a98b50bde141,
   evaluated commit 925fc5c4dbe03a865bf7fed0b0fcfd604f5b0035.
   Its odd_partition.md supplies the direct contained-token rank/unrank
   compiler, reversible history accounting and arbitrary-reference channel
   identity. Its different streaming schedule is not executed here.

The source constructions are reproduced and adapted in this submission.
Their previous verdicts are not proof premises. PCROOT-1's integration,
parameter change, limits and complete resource derivation are described
in improvement.md. The public dependency declaration names both sources.
