# Current blocked-tag bank and short typed compiler metadata

This is a complete current physical word specification, together with proof3--9, lazy-native and event-native. It selects their fully supplied direct captures, virtual zeros, unique lifetime lists and productive FWLEVEL macro. Its logical P and ALL three virtual files are unchanged. No parent verdict, measured speed, RAM oracle, unit-cost integer, uncounted zero extension or favorable tag-hit assumption is a premise.

## 1. Actual widths, types, addresses and complete field inventory

On MAINn>=2^48 retain r,cb,OWN,ROWS,WROW,HQ and source P. Set EW=ceil(sqrt n), BL=NP(EW), NB=NP(ceil(WROW/BL)), VS=NP(BL+EW)=2BL, RS=NB*VS and WM=NP(2^16r). Thus BL=Theta(sqrt n), WM=Theta(sqrt n), RS=O(n). ALL NB blocks, including padded logical positions, have BL value bits and EW tag bits plus physical stride padding. Each owner's ROWS logical rows have stride RS. The full layout and all other padded regions are proof4. No row has an additional whole-row tag.

There are TWO static payload types. LONG means the unchanged source P numerical/data fields, including labels, residues, sums, frequencies, mod-N operands, PREP/gate numeric data, source P counters/profiles and by-value arguments. They retain their prescribed n/3n-bit encoding and bit-expanded logical operations counted in C_P. SHORT means ONLY compiler-generated identities, descriptors, bindings, typed references and physical control/generator metadata listed below. Every SHORT field has WM significant bits, including leading zeros, at fixed known ordinals. The compiler's staging registers for SHORT operations have WM bits. No instruction builds an n-bit physical temporary merely to fill its high zeros.

SHORT inventory and pointwise bounds:
- family/name/returnPC/static instruction IDs are in the fixed finite source graph; lazy-native's finite inventory has fewer than B=2^20 binding names and fewer than16+3B frame fields. The fixed graph/ordinal is not an instance table.
- owner IDs<=OWN=2^16r; caller/current/parent activation IDs<FRAMECAP8r; control phase/family/depth/arity labels are bounded by the fixed syntax or O(r) source stack depth. Local/formal/free references are triples(owner,row,field), never a copied numerical value.
- logical row indices<ROWS=2^(cb+24), bit/field offsets<WROW=2^16n, row/field bounds no larger than these capacities. Array capacities, gate operand qubit indices and canonical descriptor locations obey proof3/P3--P4. A LONG P arithmetic result used as an index is already computed bit by bit in C_P. Only its bounded low WM index bits enter the physical address register; the proof of its legal index range implies its omitted high bits are zero. No scan or copy of a LONG value is omitted from the P expansion.
- bound slot/HEAD/NEXT/PREV IDs are <=HQ=2^22r2^cb, OWNER<=OWN, zero and HQ sentinels included. Every numeric descriptor k+1 has WM bits; a published qubit handle stays opaque to P arithmetic/sort keys. Entire-register references retain canonical descriptor maps.
- all physical segment/row/block/frame/wire addresses and strides are bounded by the WHOLE allocation. Proof8 has log2(2h)+1<=sig sqrt n+O(logn)+O(1); current DRAW differs by at most a fixed factor from the supplied bank-value span. In particular log2(2h)+1<64r at n>=2^48 by proof3's r>=2^24, cb<=r and n<=r^2 (or the harmless adjacent-integer ceiling version). The explicit padded interpreter scratch, captures, 8rframes and constants2^56 are included. Shifted address results and one carry/sign bit fit WM>2^16r.
- generator bit indices, block indices, public record/region offsets, capture ordinals and phase/call/return counters are bounded by those same region sizes or by their bit lengths. Actual structural pool counters use their source PUBLIC cap, not a global running-time counter. From the supplied rounded schedule, log2 PP<=er+3<2r+3, C0<=3*2^29r^5PP, Qcap=4nC0+K, K<=2^18r and e<2. Thus log2(Qcap+1)<8r. Individual FW round/chunk/REQUEST and TREE stage counters are bounded by their explicit D_i,Rc_i,Yd_i, fixed q(Y+1), pool acquisition cap or table capacity in virtual2: their defining products have at most fixed factors from these bounds. A conservative64r bits covers them. If an actual source numerical loop variable uses a larger LONG encoding it remains a P field and is bit-expanded; no such arbitrary numerical value is a SHORT payload.

These bounds use actual public capacities and permitted operations, not a distribution of addresses or successful branches. For an address sum/product/shift, its explicit maximum lies in the selected full region or bounded fixed metadata formula. Compute it with WM-bit ripple/shift streams and a carry/sign bit within that bound. No legal computation overflows. Addition, comparison, mask/shift and copy on SHORT fields agree exactly with the old zero-extended logical representation: high zeros/sign extension contribute no arithmetic change within the proved range. Signed metadata uses the same bounded two's complement with a fixed sign convention; a zero/nonnegative field is never interpreted with an incorrect sign. Ordinary P signed labels/arithmetic are LONG. This is a compiler simulation relation, not a runtime promise of free n-bit zero filling.

SHORT descriptors may share a logical row with LONG P labels; they occupy distinct typed fields. They are not truncated P numbers. Source handles are opaque canonical references, so changing their physical slot encoding changes no numerical key or logical operation. Every descriptor and caller field fits WM; proof3's original at-most12n-bit row fit remains a valid upper bound, since WM<=n on MAIN. A fixed-name access resolves directly through the counted current frame/captured reference, with no parent walk.

SHORT fields keep their fixed source logical field locations. If the abstract P field has n bits, only its low WM bits are compiler-written; its remaining logical high bits are invariantly0. Fresh-owner mismatch decodes them0; the first materialization of any shared block actually clears them; no disjoint typed neighboring field writes their positions. Repeated legal SHORT values have zero high bits. A source P bit-expanded opaque-handle copy may still read/write those high zero positions and is charged in C_P; it does not generate a free numeric operation. Reuse with another field role requires the ordinary fresh-owner epoch or explicit P writes, so no stale matching high bits are assumed zero.

## 2. Literal blocked READ/WRITE and exact decoded memory

For logical (o,i,j), form u=o*ROWS+i by concatenating the fixed row field. Let a=floor(j/BL),z=j modBL. Row base is bank_start+u*RS, block base adds a*VS, value is at z, tag bits at BL+t. All strides are powers of two. Form addresses by the actual WM-bit shifts/masks/additions, including ALL global segment offsets. No multiplication depending on quantum data, lookup table or superposed address is used.

READBIT compares the EW blocktag to ep(o) bit-serially and returns its value at z if equal, else0. It writes no value. WRITEBIT first compares the same tag. On mismatch it RAW_WRITEs EVERY BL value bit of that block to0 with one streaming pointer, THEN copies EVERY EW ep bit into its blocktag; finally it writes the desired bit. A zero payload still executes the complete clear and publication. A matching tag changes only the selected bit. All physical stride padding remains allocated and unused. No matching tag is ever published over stale values.

Decode every bit through its own blocktag. Until the first write in a fresh owner epoch, all blocks mismatch and decode0, whatever their stale values. First write materializes ALL zeros of its block before publishing the epoch; every unwritten bit of that matching block is genuinely0. Later writes preserve other bits. A different block remains logically0 independently. Induction over operations proves the last-write/fresh-zero logical memory law for EVERY allowed stale basis content. This proves refinement even when two typed fields share one block or a field crosses blocks. Owner freeing changes only the active set after its bound qubits are released; fresh epochs prevent stale resurrection.

RAW_READ is computational-basis measurement of its counted bank wire. RAW_WRITE reads the old bit, conditionally appliesX, then forgets that staged bit. On the computational-basis/CQ bank invariant these operations preserve arbitrary conditional quantum/reference states exactly under the decoded classical law. Bank tag/clear transcripts are physical refinements, not extra variables in source P's held-out-bit sigma-fields.

## 3. SHORT WORD instruction, arbitrary field alignment and zero extension

READWORD(o,i,j,L), L<=WM, may cross a constant number of BL blocks: WM/BL is bounded by an absolute fixed constant (r and sqrt n differ by at most1). For each nonempty block segment compare its tag ONCE; stream its L_segment values if equal, else stream L_segment zeros into the WM-bit SHORT buffer. WRITEWORD stages at most WM bits, then for each intersected nonempty block compare once; on miss clear ALL BL values before publishing ALL EW epoch bits, even for all-zero payload. Stream the payload in field order. Empty fields do nothing. If a field crosses a logical row boundary, split there as well; WM<=n<WROW, so at most2 rows are touched. Full tags/clears are separately executed per intersected block.

A RAW SHORT field skips tags and streams ALL WM significant bits. Every owner binding is eight such fields. Every continuation PUSH writes ALL16+3B significant control/capture words, including zero-filled unused captures, BEFORE the frame is readable. RAW NEXT/PREV/OWNER/head/virgin fields have strideWM; each role change completely writes the WM significant payload before interpretation. Padding beyond a record's significant fields is never used as a value. B=2^20 is fixed, not a function of N. CALL captures identity triples rather than by-value P numbers; LONG argument copies remain P operations. Only constant-many WM-bit buffers and ordinary source n-bit buffers are staged, so SC=O(n).

For every aligned basis memory and arbitrary staged payload, READWORD equals the sequence of source decoded bit reads. On a mismatch WRITEWORD's first payload bit materializes the whole block exactly as WRITEBIT, and following bits have a matching tag. At a block boundary the argument repeats independently. Thus the field instruction equals its ordered decoded bit writes, including preservation of neighboring LONG fields and values outside the field. It does not claim equality of the detailed RAW transcripts, only exact decoded CQ/reference channel. RAW SHORT instructions equal the same full significant bit-string representation. Injective zero extension relates the WM-bit compiler type to the original abstract metadata identities; the lexical and slot-list proofs use those identities, not a physical n-bit copying instruction.

The instruction changes no P numeric bit expansion, failure flag, source request, gate, alias identity or acceptance criterion. The no-group FWLEVEL jump remains exactly event-native's complete-block refinement. Source P bit operations are still counted individually in C_P, including LONG value arithmetic and copies.

## 4. Exact renumbering of ALL blocks

G and ep have EW bits. When G is full, pause P and visit ALL owners/rows/blocks, including unused padded blocks. Copy an active owner's old epoch into the counted RAW backup. A block matching backup keeps ALL BL values; a mismatch is completely materialized as zeros. Then set that blocktag to ownerID+1. Only after ALL its blocks are processed, set the owner's ep to ownerID+1. Inactive owner blocks/epochs are cleared. Finally G=OWN. Intermediate partial updates are not aligned points. RAW bindings, HEAD/list links, descriptor REFERENCES in captures, continuation frames and cached PC/frame fields are preserved. Descriptor values inside matching blocks remain unchanged; mismatching descriptor values already decode0.

Every active decoded bit is preserved by this blockwise procedure. Afterwards active blocktags match the new epoch, all tags<=G, and later allocations use a fresh epoch. OWN+2<2^(EW-1) is the same explicit main guard. At least2^(EW-1) outside allocations separate maintenance phases, no additive initial maintenance is charged. Renumbering allocates no owner, source or quantum wire. Total blocktag/value/epoch iterator visits are O(DRAW): NB*VS is the counted row span, tags/unused blocks included. Retain the conservative O(n^22DRAW) per-phase upper bound with every address/bit/gate charged; changed shorter fields cannot increase it. Productive owner events satisfy A<=poly(n)2^(e sqrt n), so total T_renumber<=poly(n)2^((e+sig-1)sqrt n), strictly below the main coefficient since sig=1/2. No claim that renumbering eventually never happens is used.

## 5. Full native cost, initialization and controller/slot accounting

Whole allocation gives d=O(sqrt n), and EVERY classical bit operation/gate pays full address fee d. Addressing, SHORT ripple/copy/tag/epoch operations use O(WM+EW)=O(sqrt n) bit operations. A P READBIT/WRITEBIT additionally clears at most BL=O(sqrt n) values on ANY miss. Therefore EVERY logical P bit/core operation's bounded bank/operand/gate expansion costs O(n), on all branches and stale contents. There is no favorable-hit assumption or amortization over a long initialized value.

A SHORT field touches O(1) blocks and streams O(WM) payload bits; ALL misses clear O(BL) each. Its cost is likewise O(n), including field/row/block/segment address formation. Pointer streams use bounded down-counters with O(L+log(L+1)) total bit changes for ANY initial address/carry pattern as proof9; initialization of a stream uses O(WM) paid bits. A one-bit source-P value access never incurs a full n-bit metadata copy. Its legal short coordinates are typed IDs; original LONG numerical computation that produced an index remains in C_P.

ALLOC_OWNER scans OWN=O(r) active flags, with paid pointer setup, increments/copies EW-bit epochs and writes eight SHORT bindings plus the SHORT caller field. Total O(n), excluding exact renumber separately. FREE_OWNER header costs O(n); each bound-list iteration is separately charged per wire event. A full RAW continuation frame fill has 16+3B fixed SHORT fields, so O(n); POP/direct reference lookup likewise O(n), no ancestor walk. All zero unused captures are actually written. Simple helpers have finite bounded static nesting; only counted RAW frames grow with input.

Slot ENSURE, publication bind, list INSERT/UNLINK/MOVE and low-level allocate/free use constant-many SHORT descriptor/link/head words plus the identical physical gate/reset. Every event costs O(n). Lazy-native's unique canonical map and lifetime proof is unchanged under the injective typed identity encoding. First-touch/publication binds/releases/transfers total O(C_P+(n+1)Qcap). Unknown source positions bind BEFORE SWAP; at most n+1 events per source cost O(n²), including all moves/acquisition disposal and address generation. No source state is replaced with a zero assumption.

The productive control count E=O(C_P+Qcap)+poly(n) is event-native's SAME own-work charging, including one unproductive FW tail per actual run, the terminal no-group jump, all completed REQUEST/TREE calls, failed attempts and at most one partial abort chain.
